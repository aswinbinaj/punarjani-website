Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

Learn more about our governance, volunteer participation, and initiatives.

What is Punarjani Charitable Trust?

Punarjani Charitable Trust is a registered non-profit organization based in Karunagappally, Kollam, Kerala, dedicated to awakening India's indigenous cultural roots, empowering students through skill-based education, supporting farmers via producer organizations, and running compassionate care programs for children and senior citizens.

How will my donation be utilized?

Your contributions directly fund operational materials for the Keralaayanam cultural tour, student learning kits in the Pravah program, farm equipment and processing for rural farmers, and essential medical supplies for our special education center and care home.

Who can join the Keralaayanam Expedition?

Anyone passionate about Kerala’s heritage, local ecology, folklore, and indigenous crafts is welcome to join! Volunteers, students, teachers, artists, researchers, and community elders can participate in district-level programs and flag-off gatherings.

Can schools and colleges invite the Pravah team?

Yes! Educational institutions across Kerala can register with us to host Pravah workshops covering career orientation, traditional arts, digital skills, sports, and financial literacy.

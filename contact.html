Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Common inquiries regarding donations, volunteer registration, and trust operations.

How does the direct scan donation work?

You can simply open Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, or any banking app, scan our official QR code or transfer directly to punarjanitrust8@gmail.com. You choose any amount you wish to contribute directly.

How do I get an official tax receipt?

After completing your UPI payment, tap the "Send Payment Screenshot on WhatsApp" button on this page (or message +91 9946541555) with your name and address. Our team issues official receipts promptly.

How can our school or college join Pravah (പ്രവാഹ്)?

Educational institutions can register for student training programs, coding labs, arts, and sports workshops by contacting us at +91 9946541555 or submitting the volunteer/partnership form above.

Where is the registered headquarters?

Punarjani Charitable Trust is officially registered and headquartered in Karunagappally, Kollam District, Kerala - 690518, operating socio-cultural programs state-wide across all 14 districts.
